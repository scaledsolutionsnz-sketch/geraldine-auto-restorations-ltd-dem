What We Do

Expert Auto Services

From full body-off restorations to precision panel repairs, our Geraldine workshop handles it all with hands-on care and decades of experience.

Classic Car Restoration

Full body-off restorations bringing classic and vintage vehicles back to showroom condition. We take the time to get every detail right.

Panel Beating

Skilled panel beating and reshaping to factory specification. Collision damage, dents, and structural deformation repaired with precision.

Auto Painting

High-quality spray painting and colour matching. From full resprays to spot repairs, we deliver a flawless finish that lasts.

Rust Repair

Cut out and replace rust-affected panels and chassis sections. We stop corrosion in its tracks and restore structural integrity.

Chassis Work

Structural repairs and chassis straightening using professional equipment. We restore vehicle geometry and alignment to manufacturer tolerances.

Custom Bodywork

Custom fabrication, body modifications, and one-off metalwork. From subtle custom touches to full bespoke body transformations.

Our Story

Geraldine Through and Through

Geraldine Auto Restorations Ltd is a proudly local business, built on honest workmanship and a genuine passion for vehicles. Wayne started the business with a simple goal: to deliver the kind of restoration and repair work that stands the test of time.

Nestled in the heart of South Canterbury, our workshop has become a destination for classic car owners, enthusiasts, and everyday motorists who want the job done properly. We treat every vehicle as if it were our own.
