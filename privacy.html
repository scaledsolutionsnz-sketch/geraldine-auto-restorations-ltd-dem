Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Geraldine Auto Restorations Ltd is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Name and contact details (phone, email, address)
  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, registration)
  • Details of work requested or completed
  • Payment information (processed securely)
  • Feedback and reviews submitted through our website

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide quotes and complete automotive services
  • Communicate with you about your vehicle or enquiry
  • Process payments and maintain business records
  • Improve our services based on feedback
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers (e.g. payment processors, parts suppliers) where necessary to complete your service. All third parties are required to handle your information securely.

4. Contact Form Data

Enquiries submitted through our website contact form are processed by StaticForms and forwarded to our business email. Please refer to StaticForms' privacy policy for details on their data handling practices.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no data transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Access and Correction

You have the right to access and correct any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet our legal obligations. When no longer needed, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools at this time.
